# Queue packages

- Load [queue authoring](../../.agents/skills/voucha-queue-authoring/SKILL.md) and [the queue checklist](../../docs/checklists/backend-queues.md) for queues, schedulers, payloads, retries, deduplication, backfills, or placement.
- Before adding a queue, merging queues, or waiting on another job, follow [queue boundaries](../../docs/checklists/backend-queues.md#queue-boundaries).
- Every shared-queue job sets a domain-prefixed `ordering` key. Its `concurrency` is a global cap read from DynamicConfig, never derived from `workerConcurrency`. Floodable work gets its own lane, and dispatchers get a lane separate from tasks. See [shared-queue ordering](../../docs/checklists/backend-queues.md#queue-boundaries).
- Dedicated queue domains own `config.mts`, `queues.mts`, `types.mts`, enqueue modules, and payload modules. Shared-queue producer domains own enqueue/payload modules and use the shared package's config, queue, and types. Business logic belongs in services; execution belongs in workers. Queues are safe for API/worker imports and never import `@workers/*`.
- Canonical post create/update completion may await self-reporting admission wrappers; this is API settlement, not worker completion. See [posts completion](../../docs/overview/architecture/services/README.md).
- URL-created service call sites await the queue-owned `BestEffort` admission facade after their mutation commits; topic creation's post-commit finalizer also awaits its topic-created admission. Both preserve the committed mutation and report enqueue failures once. Other entity-listener families retain their documented producer-specific ownership. `no-sync-throw-assert-on-async-enqueue` depends on these producer contracts.
- Every queue has a non-DLQ terminal recovery path: backfill, self-healing/reconciliation dispatcher, or documented accepted loss. Follow [job replayability](../../docs/requirements/platform/JOB-REPLAYABILITY.md#rules-for-new-jobs).
- Update domain docs and [queue inventory](../../docs/overview/architecture/queues/README.md) with behavior changes; use [package inventory](../../docs/overview/architecture/backend/README.md) and [worker rules](../workers/AGENTS.md).
