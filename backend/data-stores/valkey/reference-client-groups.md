# Client Groups

[Back to Valkey Data Store](README.md#client-groups)

In dev, all env vars point to the same localhost instance. `valkyries` `upsertValkeyClientByUrl()` deduplicates connections by URL, read preference, lazy-connect setting, and optional `name`. Clients that share a URL but need distinct `GlideClient` instances (e.g. `bloomValkeyClient`) pass a unique `name` to force isolation.

| Group              | Clients                                                                                       | `readFrom`                                          | Env Var                     | What Lives Here                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Eviction Policy | Persistence                  |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Session**        | `sessionValkeyClient`                                                                         | `primary`                                           | `VALKEY_SESSION_URL`        | JWT revocation flags, JWT staleness flags, passkey challenges (`passkey-challenge:`)                                                                                                                                    | `volatile-lru`  | `appendonly no`, `save ""`   |
| **Cache**          | `cacheValkeyClient`                                                                           | `preferReplica`                                     | `VALKEY_CACHE_URL`          | Entity caches, sitemap range                                                                                                                                                                                            | `allkeys-lru`   | `appendonly no`, `save ""`   |
| **Bloom**          | `bloomValkeyClient`                                                                           | `primary`                                           | `VALKEY_BLOOM_URL`          | Bloom filters and ready markers                                                                                                                                                                                         | `allkeys-lru`   | `appendonly no`, `save ""`   |
| **Rate Limiter**   | `rateLimiterValkeyClient`                                                                     | `primary`                                           | `VALKEY_RATE_LIMITER_URL`   | Rate limiters, domain crawl throttle                                                                                                                                                                                    | `volatile-ttl`  | `appendonly no`, `save ""`   |
| **Dynamic Config** | `dynamicConfigValkeyClient`, `dynamicConfigPrimaryValkeyClient`, dedicated pub/sub connection | `preferReplica`; primary for accounting uncertainty | `VALKEY_DYNAMIC_CONFIG_URL` | Feature flags, tunables, and fail-closed AI accounting uncertainty latch (`ai-usage:accounting-uncertain:<request-day>`). Ops may `UNLINK` a day key to recover after an outage; do not flush the Dynamic Config group. | `noeviction`    | `appendonly yes`, `everysec` |
| **Worker Queue**   | GlideMQ                                                                                       | `primary`                                           | `VALKEY_WORKER_QUEUE_URL`   | Job queues                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `noeviction`    | `appendonly yes`, `everysec` |

Use `primary` when reads must be strongly consistent, such as auth/session checks, rate limiting, and Bloom ready-marker/live-filter reads. `preferReplica` clients route write commands to primary automatically.
