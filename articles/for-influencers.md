# Voucha for Influencers: One Hub for All Your Referral Links

If you recommend products to an audience — on YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, a blog, a newsletter, anywhere — your referral links are scattered. Some in video descriptions. Some in tweet threads. Some in your Instagram bio link tool. Some in a pinned post. Your audience has no single place to find everything you recommend, and you have no single place to manage it.

Voucha is that single place — with trust signals that a generic link tool doesn't have.

## Your Branded Landing Page

Every Voucha user gets a landing page at their @username URL. For creators, this becomes your recommendation hub — one URL you share everywhere that shows all your referral links, organized by category.

Your landing page displays:

- **Your avatar and bio** — Consistent branding across platforms
- **All your referral links** — Organized by category (credit cards, hardware, AI tools, etc.)
- **Trust signals** — Your contribution history on Voucha: data points submitted, reviews written, community standing
- **Dynamic OG images** — When shared on social, your page generates a rich preview showing your username, top categories, and contribution count

One URL in your Twitter bio, Instagram profile, YouTube description, and email signature. All your recommendations in one place.

## Adding Links Takes Seconds

1. Type the product name — autocomplete matches against Voucha's topic database
2. Paste your affiliate URL
3. The link is automatically categorized and shown on your page

Add links for credit cards, hardware, software, appliances — any product category Voucha covers. When you stop recommending something, remove it. Your page always reflects your current recommendations.

## Trust Signals That Actually Mean Something

Here's what separates a Voucha page from a Linktree page: the context that explains why visitors should trust your links.

Your contribution history is visible on your landing page:

- How many structured data points you've submitted
- How many reviews you've written
- Your contributor tier (verified, trusted)
- Your engagement across verticals

A creator who has submitted 40 credit card data points, written 15 comparison reviews, and earned trusted status on Voucha presents a fundamentally different signal than a page with just links. Visitors can see that your recommendations come from someone who actually uses and evaluates the products they recommend.

## Click Analytics

Every referral link on your landing page is tracked:

- **Clicks per link** — Which products your audience engages with most
- **Trends over time** — Daily, weekly, and monthly click patterns
- **Source attribution** — Where your visitors come from (Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, direct)
- **Visitor count** — Total and unique page views

These analytics tell you which product categories your audience cares about, which platforms drive the most traffic, and which links to feature more prominently.

## Optimized for Every Platform

- **Twitter/X** — Large image card with your avatar, bio, and top categories
- **Instagram** — Works as your "link in bio" URL
- **LinkedIn** — Professional OG tags for B2B-focused recommendations
- **Facebook** — Full Open Graph support with dynamic preview images

When you share your @username URL, people see a rich, branded preview — not a generic link.

## How Contributions Build Your Referral Priority

Here's the mechanism that makes Voucha uniquely valuable for creators: the social graph.

When a logged-in Voucha user visits any topic page, referral links are ranked by trust. Links from people the visitor follows appear above links from strangers. Links from contributors with strong trust histories appear above accounts with minimal activity.

Your referral links aren't just on your landing page — they're prioritized across the entire platform for everyone who follows you. If someone follows you on Voucha and then visits a credit card topic page, your referral link for that card appears before links from people they don't know.

The way to maximize this: contribute to the platform. Submit data points. Write reviews. The more useful you are to the community, the more visible your links become for your followers. Creators who contribute genuine product intelligence earn more referral visibility than those who just post links.

## Getting Started

1. **Create your account** and choose a recognizable username
2. **Add your referral links** — Start with your most-recommended products
3. **Write a bio** that tells visitors who you are and why they should trust your recommendations
4. **Submit data points** for products you've used — this builds your trust profile immediately
5. **Write a comparison review** in your area of expertise
6. **Share your @username URL** everywhere — social bios, video descriptions, email signatures
7. **Check analytics** to see which links get engagement and where traffic comes from

Your audience already trusts your recommendations. Voucha gives that trust a clean home, with the data to back it up.
