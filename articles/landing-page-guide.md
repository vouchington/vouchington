# Create Your Referral Landing Page in 60 Seconds

Your Voucha landing page is a shareable @username URL where you put your referral links, your recommendations, and a bit about who you are — so your audience can find everything in one place.

Think of it as a link-in-bio page built specifically for product recommendations — with trust signals, click analytics, and social graph integration that generic link tools don't have.

## What a Landing Page Is

Every Voucha user gets a landing page at their @username URL. It shows:

- Your display name and avatar
- A short bio
- Your referral links, organized by category
- Trust signals from your platform contributions (data points submitted, reviews written, community standing)

When someone visits your page, they see your recommendations alongside the credibility you've built on the platform. If they're logged into Voucha, your links get social graph priority — meaning your referral links rank higher for people who follow you, even on topic pages outside your landing page.

## Why It's Worth Having

If you recommend credit cards on Twitter, hardware on YouTube, and AI tools on a blog, your audience has no single place to find everything. A Voucha landing page gives you that URL. And because it's backed by structured data and community trust, your recommendations carry more weight than links in a Google Doc or a Linktree page.

The growth loop is simple:

1. You create your landing page with referral links
2. You share the URL on social media, in forums, in email signatures
3. Visitors arrive and see your curated recommendations
4. Some click through and use your referral links
5. Some sign up for Voucha and create their own pages

Your referral links earn you the same value they would anywhere else — but with trust signals that actually increase conversion.

## Step-by-Step: Creating Your Page

### 1. Choose Your Display Name and Avatar

Navigate to your profile settings. Set a display name — this is the first thing visitors see on your landing page. Upload an avatar if you haven't already. Make them recognizable if your audience knows you from other platforms.

### 2. Write a Short Bio

Your bio appears below your avatar. Keep it to one or two sentences — who you are and what you recommend. For example:

- "Software engineer. I track every credit card app and hardware purchase."
- "Travel hacker since 2019. 2M+ points earned across programs."
- "AI researcher comparing every coding assistant on the market."

The bio gives visitors a reason to trust your picks.

### 3. Add Your Referral Links

This is the core of the page. To add a link:

1. Click "Add Referral Link"
2. Start typing the product name — autocomplete matches against Voucha topics
3. Paste your affiliate or referral URL
4. The link is automatically categorized based on the topic

Add as many links as you want. They're organized by category on your page (credit cards, hardware, AI tools, etc.), so visitors can find what they're looking for quickly.

### 4. Preview and Publish

Review how your page looks, then publish. Changes take effect immediately.

## Tips for Getting More Clicks

### Be an active contributor first

Your page displays trust signals based on your platform activity. Visitors who see that you've submitted 50 credit card data points and written detailed comparison reviews are more likely to click your links than visitors who see a blank page with just URLs.

The most effective approach: contribute on Voucha first, then let your track record speak for your recommendations.

### Organize by category

If you have links across multiple verticals, the automatic category organization helps visitors find what they need. Someone coming from a credit card discussion goes straight to your credit card recommendations. Someone from a hardware forum finds your hardware picks.

### Add a personal note to each link

When adding referral links, include a brief note about why you recommend the product. "My daily driver for 18 months — the travel perks alone justify the annual fee" is more compelling than just a link.

### Keep links current

Remove links for products you no longer recommend or that have expired offers. Stale links erode trust. Visitors who click through to find a dead offer won't come back.

## Where to Share Your Page

**Twitter/X bio.** Replace your generic link with your Voucha @username URL. Every tweet about a product becomes an implicit invitation to check your landing page.

**Instagram link in bio.** Your Voucha landing page replaces Linktree for product recommendations — one URL with all your links, backed by community trust signals.

**LinkedIn profile.** For professional recommendations (AI tools, SaaS products, productivity hardware), your landing page in your LinkedIn contact info reaches an audience that makes real purchasing decisions.

**Email signatures.** Add your @username URL to your email signature. Colleagues and contacts who value your product opinions can browse your recommendations any time.

**Forum signatures.** If you're active in communities like r/churning, hardware forums, or AI discussion groups, your landing page gives fellow members one place to find all your recommendations.

## Analytics

Your landing page includes click analytics so you can see what's working:

- **Clicks per link** — Which referral links get the most engagement
- **Trends over time** — Daily, weekly, and monthly click patterns
- **Source attribution** — Where your visitors are coming from (Twitter, Reddit, direct, etc.)
- **Visitor count** — Total and unique page views

Use these to understand which products your audience cares about most, which sharing channels drive the most traffic, and how your page performance changes over time.

## The Social Graph Difference

Here's what makes a Voucha landing page different from any other link-in-bio tool: when a logged-in Voucha user visits any topic page on the platform, referral links from people they follow rank higher than links from strangers.

Your landing page isn't just a static list of links — it's the foundation of a referral network where your followers see your links prioritized across the entire platform.

The more followers you build, the more surfaces your links appear on with priority ranking. And because people follow contributors whose data points and reviews they actually find useful, the system naturally aligns incentive with quality.

Build trust through contributions. Earn followers through quality. Let the social graph do the rest.
