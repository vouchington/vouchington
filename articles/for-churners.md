# Voucha for Churners: Real Approval Odds from Real Applicants

You've got a spreadsheet, a mental model of issuer velocity rules, and strong opinions about which blogs are actually useful versus which ones are just pushing the highest-commission card. What you don't have is a clean way to see real approval odds — not a blogger's estimate, but numbers from actual applicants with profiles like yours.

That's the gap Voucha fills.

## Real Data from Real Applicants, Not Blog Estimates

Every credit card application outcome on Voucha is a structured data point. Not a Reddit comment you have to parse. Each one captures:

- Card name (canonical, autocompleted — no abbreviation ambiguity)
- Result (approved / denied / pending / counter-offer)
- Credit score range
- Stated income range
- Existing relationship with issuer
- Hard inquiries (12 months)
- Cards opened (24 months)
- Credit limit granted
- Application method
- Application date
- Free-text notes

These fields are typed and queryable. You can filter by card, score range, inquiry count, and 5/24 status. No more scrolling six months of Reddit Daily Question threads to find a DP that matches your profile.

When a card has enough data points (minimum 10 for approval rates), Voucha calculates aggregate statistics with confidence intervals:

- Approval rate by credit score range
- Approval rate by inquiry count
- Average credit limit by score range
- Approval trend over time (is the issuer tightening or loosening?)

The difference between "approval odds look good according to a blog post from March" and "78% of 340 applicants in the 750-799 range with 3-4 recent inquiries were approved in the last 90 days" is the difference between guessing and deciding.

## Comparison Reviews of Sign-Up Bonuses

SUB values change. The best travel card offer last quarter might not be this quarter. Voucha comparison reviews let you directly compare cards across the dimensions that actually matter:

- Sign-up bonus value
- Minimum spending requirement difficulty
- Annual fee vs first-year value
- Ongoing earning rate
- Transfer partner flexibility
- Retention offer likelihood

Comparison reviews require differentiation — you can't rate everything 5 stars. Because they're structured with sub-ratings and winner picks, they're scannable in ways a 2,000-word blog post isn't.

## Referral Links from People You Trust

When you're ready to apply, every card on Voucha shows referral links ranked by trust. If you follow someone whose data points you've found accurate, their referral link appears above links from strangers. They get the referral bonus. You get the same SUB you'd get from any referral link.

The people who contribute the most useful data to the community get the most referral visibility. Incentives align: contribute good data, build trust, earn referral clicks.

## Data Quality You Can Actually Trust

Not all data points are equal, and Voucha doesn't treat them that way.

New accounts have a verification period before their data points carry full weight in aggregates. Contributors who've submitted multiple accurate data points earn verified and trusted status, which increases their weight in calculations.

The approval rates you see are weighted toward data from established contributors — not inflated by fresh accounts submitting dubious DPs.

## What It Looks Like in Practice

You're planning your next application. You open the card's page on Voucha and see:

1. **Approval rate**: 81% for 750+ scores, 64% for 700-749, based on 420 data points
2. **5/24 data**: 12 applicants at 5/24 — 3 approved, 9 denied. This one is risky above 5/24.
3. **Recent trend**: Approval rate up 4% in the last 60 days (possibly a push for new accounts this quarter)
4. **Average CL**: $14,200 median for 750+ scores
5. **Comparison review**: Side-by-side against the main competitor card, with dimensional sub-ratings
6. **Referral link**: From someone you follow who was approved last month with a similar profile

No digging through threads. No outdated blog posts. No guessing.

If you have data points to contribute — approvals, denials, retention offers, product changes — Voucha is where that data does the most good. For the community, and for the referral priority you build by contributing it.
