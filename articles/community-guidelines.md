# Community Guidelines

Voucha is a vouched community for purchase and ownership decisions. These guidelines explain what belongs here, what does not, and how reports are reviewed.

## What Belongs On Voucha

Use Voucha to share and compare real experience with products, services, rewards, referral programs, communities, news, podcasts, videos, reviews, and data points.

Good contributions are specific and useful. They can include:

- Reviews based on your own experience
- Data points from real applications, purchases, accounts, or ownership
- Discussions that help people evaluate a product or service
- Referral links that follow the referral-link rules
- Topic labels, related links, and community-curated lists
- Reports that help moderators review spam, harassment, fraud, or illegal material

## Prohibited Material

Do not post, share, promote, or coordinate:

- Hate speech, harassment, threats, or targeted abuse
- Sexually explicit material or graphic violence
- Spam, fake engagement, repetitive low-quality posts, or undisclosed self-promotion
- Fabricated reviews, fake data points, false ownership claims, or manipulated outcomes
- Buying, selling, trading, or marketplace listings
- Illegal material, fraud, copyright infringement, or illegal goods promotion
- Partisan political advocacy or unsupported political claims
- Content unrelated to purchase or ownership decisions

Voucha may remove prohibited material and may limit, suspend, or terminate accounts that violate these guidelines or the Terms of Service.

## Reviews And Data Points

Reviews and data points should reflect your real experience. Do not invent outcomes, copy someone else's experience as your own, or submit information you know is false.

If you have a material connection to a product, issuer, merchant, creator, community, or referral program, disclose it where it matters.

## Referral Links

Referral links should help people compare real options. Do not use misleading labels, hide material terms, impersonate another person, or create fake activity around a link.

Official Voucha links are labeled as official platform links. User referral links remain user submissions.

## Community Conduct

Disagree with claims, not identities. Keep criticism specific to the product, service, data point, review, or moderation decision.

Do not target people because of protected characteristics, private personal information, or off-platform disputes. Do not organize harassment or mass-report campaigns.

## Reporting And Review

Signed-in users can report posts, comments, user profiles, RSS feed items, and domains. Reports create moderation queue items for review. They do not automatically remove material.

Moderators may use automated checks, AI-assisted judgement, community moderation tools, and human review. AI recommendations are advisory. Human moderators make final enforcement decisions for escalated cases.

## Copyright Reports

Copyright reports follow a separate legal process. They are not ordinary moderation reports. When
the US copyright process is active, anyone can submit a structured notice through the form, with or
without signing in, or send a notice by email to the designated agent, and an affected poster can
appeal or submit a counter-notice. CAPTCHA helps prevent abuse. It does not decide whether a claim
is legally valid. A copyright moderator reviews every notice. An AI agent may assist, but it does
not decide.

Do not submit false, misleading, or abusive copyright notices or counter-notices. Voucha may take
account action for repeated abuse after human review. Repeat infringement is handled under the
repeat-infringer policy in Section 10 of the Terms of Service. Accepted copyright case records are
visible to signed-in members. When a signed-in member filed the notice, a record shows that
member's current public profile if it still exists. Records do not reveal legal names, contact
details, signatures, raw correspondence, evidence, moderator rationale, or agent analysis.

See [Copyright and the DMCA on Voucha](./copyright-and-dmca.md) and
[How Copyright Complaints Work](./copyright-complaints.md) for more detail on the copyright process.

## Appeals

Members may appeal eligible warnings, bans, and post removals. Verified topic representatives may file disputes about reviews they believe are legally or factually problematic.

See [How Moderation Works](./how-moderation-works.md), the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md), and the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for more detail.
