# How Voucha Makes Money

Any site that recommends things should say how it gets paid. Here is how it works at Voucha.

## Three sources

**Paid memberships.** Voucha is free to join and explore. Paid plans, called Plus and Pro, add things like higher contribution limits and tools for communities. See the [plans page](/plans) for the current list.

**Referral links.** Some programs pay when someone signs up through a link. Voucha has [referral programs](/referral-programs) for these, and both members and Voucha itself can attach links to them.

**Ads, as policy.** Free accounts will see ads. Paid members will not. Ads are not live yet, so this is our policy and not a feature you can use today.

Neither ads nor referral links change rankings or trust. Nobody can pay to rank higher.

## How referral links are ordered

When you look at a referral program, you see a list of links. The order depends on your relationship to each link's owner.

1. Voucha's official links, labeled as official.
2. Mutual follows.
3. People you follow.
4. People who referred you.
5. Authors you rated positively.
6. Everyone else.

Inside a group, people who have posted data points and reviews on that program come first. Links from anyone you have muted or blocked are left out. This is the idea behind "referral links from your inner circle": the person whose link you use is someone you already trust.

Voucha's own link sits at the top in a group of its own, so you always know which one is ours. It shows an Official Voucha badge. Pick someone else's link if you prefer. The list is there so you can choose.

The longer version for credit cards is in [Credit Card Referral Links Explained](./credit-card-referral-links-explained.md).

## What links do not touch

Referral links do not feed into vote weight, and vote weight is the number that decides how much a person's vote counts. A post does not rank higher because its author owns a link.

Paid membership does feed into vote weight. A Plus or Pro member's vote counts for more than a free member's, all else equal. That is a real tradeoff, and we would rather you read it here than find it later. Account age, verified sign-in methods and identity verification feed in as well, and abuse penalties push the weight down. See [How Trust Works on Voucha](./trust-signals-explained.md).

## Official accounts cannot vote

Staff, administrators, investors and automated accounts are marked as official or system accounts. They cannot vote on posts, topics, or domains. They cannot write reviews or data points. They cannot endorse a referral link personally. The one exception is the official Voucha link, which is admin-only and visibly labeled.

Staff who want to take part as ordinary members use separate personal accounts. Those accounts must disclose any material connection to what they post about.

## Disclosure

Every referral link shows its owner. A link that belongs to Voucha shows the official badge. If a member would earn something when you use their link, you can see who they are, what else they have posted, and whether they reviewed the program. If you do not trust them, use another link or none.

## What we do not do

We do not sell positions in the rankings. A company cannot pay to make its product look better, an advertiser cannot buy trust, and a link owner cannot pay to move up.
