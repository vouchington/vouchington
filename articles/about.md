# About Voucha

Voucha is a social trust network. The idea is simple: the people you trust should shape what you see, read, and buy — not anonymous strangers, paid rankings, or an AI guessing from the whole gamed internet. The name says it — people **vouch** for things, with their reputation attached.

## Why We Built It

The internet broke trust. Review sites get paid by the products they rank. Amazon reviews are gamed. Reddit has honest takes, but they're buried in threads you can't search or add up. And now AI confidently repeats all of it back to you.

When every "best of" list might be an ad, and every five-star rating might be fake, how do you know what's real?

## How Voucha Works

You build a circle of people and sources you trust, and everything flows through it.

**Community-curated sources.** Follow the news sites, podcasts, and YouTube channels your friends trust — and discover new ones through the community. Review, follow, and share the sources worth your time.

**Reviews of anything.** Real reviews and real numbers — credit cards, hardware, AI tools, cars — from people who actually used them. Not affiliates chasing a commission. Not anonymous accounts from nowhere.

**Referral links from friends, not strangers.** When someone you trust recommends a product and you apply or buy through their link, they get the referral credit. Your circle's recommendations stay inside your circle.

**Your own communities.** Start a space around what you care about — a niche topic, a group of people, a shared interest — with trust and moderation built in from day one.

**A personal AI (coming soon).** We're building an AI that only answers from the websites and people you trust, not the whole gamed internet.

## The One Mechanism Underneath: Trust

The more your judgment lines up with the community over time, the more your vouch counts. People you follow count more to you than strangers. There's no karma for memes. There's no pay-to-rank.

Trust is contextual: if you consistently submit accurate data points about credit cards, your credit card reviews carry more weight. It builds by category, by behavior, by track record.

## What Makes It Different

Review sites serve advertisers, not you. Reddit has honest takes but you can't aggregate them. Amazon's review system is gamed. Forums are fragmented.

Voucha gives you structured data you can actually compare, social trust that makes sources accountable, and a social graph that surfaces your friends' recommendations first.

Built for credit cards today. Hardware, AI tools, cars, and more on the same foundation.

## What We Believe

Every decision you make should be backed by people you trust — not reviews you can't verify, content shaped by ad revenue, or marketing from whoever's selling to you.

Real recommendations, from real people, weighted by trust.
