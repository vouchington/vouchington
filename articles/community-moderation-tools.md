# Community Moderation Tools on Voucha

Running a community well means being able to handle problems at the right level — without turning every disagreement into a platform-wide enforcement case. Voucha gives community owners and moderators a practical set of tools to keep their space useful and on-topic.

## Triage Queue

You can require post approval before content appears in your community. The queue lets you approve posts, reject them with a reason, or unpublish posts that were previously approved. Community-scoped reports for posts and comments appear in your moderation area without exposing the reporter's identity.

## Bans and Raid Mode

Community bans remove a user's ability to participate in your community. Bans can be lifted later, and the history is preserved so you have context for future decisions.

Raid-mode restrictions let you temporarily tighten participation during coordinated abuse. These restrictions are scoped to your community and can be lifted once the situation has passed.

## Warnings, Notes, and Modmail

Warnings give users a formal notice without removing their account or content — useful when you want someone to adjust their behavior before anything escalates. Moderator notes are private context you can attach to users for future reference. Modmail gives users and moderators a community-scoped messaging path for discussing moderation questions.

## Automod Prompts

You can create custom AI moderation prompts for your community, simulate how a prompt would behave before enabling it, and configure what happens when it flags a post. An automod prompt can either flag content for moderator review or unpublish a community post automatically, depending on how you configure it.

## Community Modlog

Every moderator action is recorded in the modlog — who acted, what they did, and whether it was a human or an automated system like `automod`. This gives owners and moderators a clear audit trail.

Community moderation is intentionally scoped to your community. Site-wide suspensions, global post clearance, and domain blocking remain platform-level tools.
