# News Source Eligibility

Voucha is built around original content from people and sources with something genuine to say. Here's what we look for when adding a news source.

## What We Accept

- **Original content** — either from the creator directly (companies like OpenAI or Anthropic publishing their own research, for example) or from independent bloggers and writers.
- **Authority** — either established expertise in a field, or a track record of being trusted by people (commonly shared on Reddit, Twitter, Hacker News, and similar communities).
- **An RSS feed** — sources must publish an RSS feed so we can ingest updates.

## What We Don't Accept

- **Mainstream wire services** — no Associated Press or syndicated content. Voucha isn't trying to be a general news aggregator, though you're welcome to post links to mainstream outlets yourself.
- **Paywalled sites** — sites that put any posts behind a paywall (including metered paywalls like Medium) aren't eligible.
- **Sites with excessive ads** — if the ads significantly degrade the reading experience, we don't add the source.
- **AI-generated content** — sources that publish AI-generated writing aren't eligible.
- **Illegal or NSFW content.**

## Submitting Your Own Site

You can submit your own site — but only if it already has demonstrated authority. If your site is newer or smaller, we recommend adding it to [Kagi Small Web](https://github.com/kagisearch/smallweb), which Voucha also imports from.

## Disagreeing With a Source Being Listed

If you think a source shouldn't be on Voucha, go to that source's topic page and write a review. Sources with lower community ratings receive lower rankings.
