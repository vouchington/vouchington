# Voucha for Curators

Some people keep a list that is better than anything a site will show you. A spreadsheet of documentaries. A playlist of 4,000 videos. A folder of feeds that took years to gather. Hacker News threads this week were full of them, usually followed by a line like "but nobody sees it."

A list with no readers is a notebook. To get an audience you need a place where people already gather.

## A community is that place

On Voucha, a [community](https://voucha.ai/communities) is a group around a subject with its own members and moderators. Make one for your subject, or offer your list to one that exists. See [Voucha for Community Creators](./for-community-creators.md) for setup, roles and how people join.

## Community lists

Owners and moderators can keep [community-curated lists](https://voucha.ai/communities?has_list_items=true). A list can hold:

- topics
- sources, meaning RSS feeds such as blogs, YouTube channels and podcasts
- posts
- domains
- URLs

Members open the list and see what you picked. Anyone can import a community list's feeds into their own personal list, so your picks go where they read.

## Bring your feeds

If your collection already exists as feeds, import it. Voucha accepts OPML, XML and CSV files on the import page for channels, podcasts and news sources. Export works the same way. Your own list stays yours.

If it exists as a spreadsheet of links, add them as posts or URLs. Add a sentence on why you chose each one. That sentence is the part nobody else has.

## Why people will follow you

A shared list is only as good as the person who made it. People can follow you, read what you post, and see how others voted on your posts. If your picks hold up, your circle grows. If they do not, people mute you. Neither happens by accident, and neither happens in secret. See [Trust Is the Infrastructure](./community-trust-how-it-works.md).

## What we do not do

We do not rewrite your picks into summaries, and we do not bury the source. Voucha links straight to the source.

## Where to start

1. Pick the one list you are proudest of.
2. Create a community or join one that fits.
3. Add the list to the community's lists.
4. Post one pick a week, with a sentence about why.
