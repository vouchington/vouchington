# How Product Comparisons Work on Voucha

When you research a card, a program or a piece of hardware, you want two things. What do people who own it say, and where do you buy or sign up? Voucha keeps both next to each other.

## Topics

Everything you can compare is a [topic](https://voucha.ai/topics). Credit cards, rewards programs, referral programs, banks, retailers and spending categories are all topics. A topic has its own page, a name other people can search for, and the reviews and posts people attach to it.

Retailers are topics too. A retailer topic records the countries it operates in. Because a retailer is a topic, you can read what members say about it just as you would for a product.

## Data points and reviews

Two kinds of posts do most of the work.

- **Reviews** are written assessments with ratings.
- **Data points** are specific facts in typed fields, such as a card approval with a score range and a credit limit.

Typed fields can be counted. "Approved with a 750 score and two recent inquiries" is a number you can add up. A paragraph on a forum is not. For how that works, see [Real Numbers From Real People](./data-points-explained.md) and [Why You Can Trust Reviews on Voucha](./reviews-you-can-trust.md).

## Trust weighting

Not every submission counts the same. A vote is weighted by who cast it. Account age, verified sign-in methods, identity verification and penalties for abuse all feed in. A day-old account carries little weight. See [How Trust Works on Voucha](./trust-signals-explained.md).

## Where to sign up

Referral programs are topics, and members attach links to them. You see links ordered by your relationship to each owner: mutual follows first, then people you follow, then everyone else. Voucha's own link is labeled as official. Links do not change how a program or card ranks. [How Voucha Makes Money](./how-voucha-makes-money.md) explains the details, and [Credit Card Referral Links Explained](./credit-card-referral-links-explained.md) covers cards.

## What this page does not cover

Voucha does not track prices, stock levels or product variants by retailer. Check the retailer for those, then come back for what owners report.
