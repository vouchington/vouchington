# Getting Started with Voucha: Your First 5 Minutes

Voucha is designed to be useful from the moment you sign up. This guide walks you through the basics — from creating your account to submitting your first data point — so you can start getting value right away.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head to Voucha and sign up with email or OAuth (Google, GitHub). Choose a username — this becomes your @username handle and the URL for your landing page. Pick something recognizable if you plan to share your page publicly.

## Step 2: Pick Your Interests

After creating your account, you'll select the topics you care about. These drive your news feed and determine what surfaces first.

Topics span a range of categories:

- **Credit cards** — Chase Sapphire Preferred, Amex Gold, Capital One Venture, and hundreds more
- **Hardware** — GPUs, CPUs, laptops, networking gear, peripherals
- **AI tools** — Coding assistants, image generators, LLMs, workflow automation
- **More categories** launching over time

Follow as many or as few as you like. You can always adjust later from your profile settings.

## Step 3: Follow People You Trust

Voucha uses a social graph to prioritize content from people you follow. This is one of the most useful parts of the platform — your feed, your search results, and even the referral links you see are shaped by the people you've chosen to trust.

Follow people whose judgment you respect: friends, community members whose data is consistently helpful, reviewers whose perspective matches your own. The more you follow, the more personalized your experience becomes.

## Step 4: Browse Your Feed

Your feed combines two streams.

**Topic feed** — News and discussion related to the topics you follow. Sources are trust-scored, so the community's collective judgment determines which domains surface highest. High-quality sources rise; spam and low-effort content sinks.

**Social feed** — Posts, reviews, and data points from people you follow. When someone in your network submits a credit card approval result or publishes a hardware review, it appears here.

The feed is not algorithmic in the engagement-farming sense. It's chronological within trust-weighted tiers. Recent content from trusted sources appears first.

## Step 5: Read a Review

Reviews on Voucha are structurally different from what you'll find elsewhere. Each one includes:

- **Sub-ratings** — Not just an overall score, but dimensional ratings (rewards value, build quality, ease of use) that let you compare products on the things you actually care about
- **Duration of use** — How long the reviewer has actually used the product, so you can weigh a two-year owner's perspective differently from a two-week impression
- **Confidence level** — The reviewer's self-assessed confidence in their evaluation
- **"Compared to" link** — The product they're implicitly benchmarking against
- **"Best for" tags** — Who benefits most from this product (frequent travelers, gamers, solo developers)

**Comparison reviews** go further — they put two or more products against each other with per-dimension winner picks and conditional recommendations ("If you need X, pick A; if you need Y, pick B").

## Step 6: Understand Data Points

Data Points are the foundation of Voucha. They're structured records that users submit about real-world outcomes — credit card application results, hardware ownership experiences, AI tool usage patterns.

On any topic page, you'll see aggregates built from these submissions:

- **Credit cards**: Approval rates by credit score range, average credit limits, application method breakdowns
- **Hardware**: Failure rates by ownership duration, real-world performance metrics, recommendation rates
- **AI tools**: Usage duration distributions, switch patterns between tools, ratings by use case

These aggregates show confidence intervals based on sample size. Small datasets display wide intervals to signal uncertainty. Topics with fewer submissions than the minimum threshold show "Not enough data yet" rather than misleading statistics.

## Step 7: Submit Your First Data Point

Contributing is what makes Voucha work. Every Data Point you submit improves the picture for everyone who comes after you.

To submit one:

1. Navigate to the relevant topic page (or create a new post)
2. Select "Add Data Point"
3. Fill in the structured fields. Required fields vary by category — for a credit card, you'll need the card name, application result, and credit score range at minimum
4. Add optional fields for richer data (income range, number of recent inquiries, application method)
5. Include any free-text notes for context
6. Submit

Your Data Point is immediately included in the topic's aggregate calculations. New accounts have a short aging period before their contributions carry full weight in aggregates.

## Step 8: Create Your Landing Page

Your landing page is your personal hub on Voucha — a shareable @username URL where you can curate your referral links, showcase your reviews, and build your reputation.

Setting one up takes about 60 seconds:

1. Go to your profile settings
2. Add a display name and optional bio
3. Add referral links — type a product name and autocomplete finds the right topic
4. Preview and publish

Your landing page works as a "link in bio" replacement. Put the URL in your Twitter bio, Instagram profile, LinkedIn, email signature, or anywhere you share recommendations. When someone visits your page:

- They see your curated referral links organized by category
- Links from people you follow rank higher for logged-in visitors
- Click analytics show you which links get engagement and where traffic comes from

## What's Next

Once you've got the basics down, here are ways to go deeper.

**Vote on everything.** Votes drive the trust system. Upvote helpful data, quality reviews, and reliable news sources. Downvote spam, low-effort content, and unreliable domains. Your votes contribute to the community's collective judgment.

**Write a review.** If you have experience with a product, write a structured review. The sub-rating system and comparison review format let you contribute nuanced analysis that goes well beyond a star rating.

**Join the discussion.** Comment on posts, engage with other users' reviews, and participate in topic-level conversations. The social graph means your engagement builds connections that improve your feed over time.

**Explore other categories.** Even if you came to Voucha for credit cards, check out the hardware or AI tools sections. The same trust infrastructure works across every category, and you might find useful data in areas you hadn't thought about.

Voucha gets more valuable the more you use it — and the more you contribute. Welcome aboard.
