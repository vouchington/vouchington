# What Happens When Content Is Removed on Voucha

If your content is removed — or if you're wondering why you can't see something — here's a plain explanation of how that works.

## Automated Rejection

Every new post goes through spam detection and OpenAI moderation before it's approved. If either system flags the post, it's rejected by the clearance pipeline. You won't see it in any feed. Automated decisions are audited under the `automod` system user.

## Community Removal

A community moderator can reject a pending post or unpublish an approved post from their community. If the removal is community-scoped, the post may still exist elsewhere on Voucha — it's just no longer visible within that specific community.

## Warnings and Bans

Moderators can issue warnings when a user needs notice but their content doesn't need to be removed. Community moderators can also ban a user from their community. A community ban does not automatically suspend your global Voucha account — it only affects participation in that community.

## Contacting Moderators

For community-scoped removals, you can reach out to moderators through modmail if that community has modmail available.

## Reports and Disputes

Reports help moderators find problems, but a report alone does not automatically remove content. A human or moderation system reviews it first.

Verified representatives — a brand or issuer, for example — can file review disputes for eligible reviews if they have legal or factual grounds. Those disputes are reviewed by a human before any response is sent.

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was wrong, you can appeal it. Staff review appeals within 72 hours.

- `/my/warnings` — appeal user warnings
- `/my/bans` — appeal community bans
- `/my/removed-posts` — view and appeal community post removals
- `/my/appeals` — track the status of all your appeals

You'll receive a notification when you receive a warning, community ban, or platform-moderated post removal.
