# Voucha for Hardware Enthusiasts: Real Failure Rates from Real Owners

You already know that spec sheets don't tell the full story. A GPU's clock speed matters less than its sustained performance under load. A power supply's wattage rating matters less than its behavior at five years. An SSD's sequential read speed matters less than its consistency under mixed workloads.

The data that actually matters for hardware decisions — long-term reliability, real-world performance, failure patterns — comes from owners, not manufacturers. Voucha is built to collect, structure, and surface that data.

## Real-World Performance from Actual Owners

Professional hardware reviews provide controlled benchmarks: consistent test rigs, specific game settings, standardized workloads. Useful for relative comparison, but they don't show you the variance of real-world usage.

Owner-reported performance data captures what benchmarks can't:

- FPS in your specific game at your specific settings and resolution
- Sustained transfer speeds under real workloads, not peak sequential reads
- Thermal performance in actual cases with actual airflow configurations
- Noise levels in real rooms, not anechoic chambers
- Battery life under actual usage patterns, not manufacturer test loops

Voucha data points include structured fields for these metrics — FPS, temperatures, battery life, transfer speeds — submitted by owners with their specific configurations documented. Aggregated across hundreds of owners, the distribution of real-world performance is more useful than any single reviewer's test results.

## Failure Rate Tracking Over Time

This is the data no review site provides: what percentage of units fail, when, and how.

Professional reviewers test hardware for days or weeks. They can assess performance, build quality, and features. They can't assess reliability because they don't own the hardware for years.

Voucha tracks failure data through structured data points:

- Ownership duration at time of report
- Whether a failure occurred
- Failure type (GPU artifacts, fan failure, capacitor bulge, etc.)
- Whether the unit was replaced under warranty

Aggregated into longevity curves and failure rate calculations, this data reveals patterns invisible to any other source:

- "8% of Brand X 4080s report artifacts within 18 months"
- "Brand Y PSUs show a 3% capacitor failure rate after 3 years, vs 1% industry average"
- "No reported failures on this NVMe model across 240 data points averaging 14 months of ownership"

Confidence intervals communicate uncertainty honestly. A failure rate from 20 data points has a wide interval. A failure rate from 500 data points is statistically meaningful. You can see the difference at a glance.

## Trust-Scored Review Sources

The hardware review landscape is crowded. Some reviewers do thorough, independent testing. Others repurpose manufacturer benchmarks with minimal original work. Some are sponsored without adequate disclosure.

Voucha applies trust scoring to news and review sources based on community voting. Consistently reliable sources accumulate trust. When you browse hardware news and reviews on Voucha, the sources you see have been filtered through community assessment — not just algorithm-ranked by clicks.

This doesn't replace your own judgment about which reviewers you trust. It augments it with a community signal that helps you discover reliable sources you might not know and avoid low-quality ones.

## Comparison Reviews: GPU vs GPU, CPU vs CPU

Hardware comparison reviews on Voucha are structured for direct comparison with dimensional sub-ratings:

- Build quality
- Performance
- Value for money
- Noise / thermals
- Software / drivers
- Longevity

Each dimension gets a separate rating, and comparison reviews require a winner pick per dimension. "GPU A wins on raw performance, but GPU B wins on thermals and noise" is more useful than a single number, and the structured format makes these comparisons scannable across multiple reviews.

"Best for" tags categorize recommendations by use case — gamers at 1440p, content creators, ML training, quiet builds — so you can filter for reviews from people with your priorities.

## Community-Verified Compatibility

Hardware compatibility is one of the most frustrating parts of building systems. Will this RAM run at rated speed on this motherboard? Does this GPU fit in this case with that cooler? Does this PSU have the right connectors?

Manufacturer compatibility lists are incomplete or outdated. Forum threads have scattered reports. Voucha's structured data points capture:

- Confirmed working configurations (motherboard + RAM + settings)
- Confirmed compatibility issues
- Workarounds and BIOS settings that resolved problems

Aggregated and weighted by contributor trust, this builds a community-verified compatibility database that grows with every contribution.

- **When you buy**: Trust-ranked referral links from people in your network
