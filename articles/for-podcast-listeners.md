# Voucha for Podcast Listeners

Most podcast apps hand you a chart and a "for you" row. Charts reward whoever markets best. The "for you" row learns from what you played and shrinks. Neither tells you what a friend with good taste would tell you: start with episode 40, skip the first season.

Voucha treats a podcast as an RSS feed, because that is what it is. You choose the shows. People you trust point you to more.

## Follow shows

Open your podcasts page and add a show with its RSS address. Voucha lists new episodes in one place. Shows you follow stay yours: you can import and export the whole list as OPML, so moving to another app or back takes a minute. For why that matters, see [RSS Is Back](./rss-feeds-comeback.md).

## Pick up where you stopped

Voucha remembers your playback position in an episode. Stop on a bus, continue at home. The position is yours and is saved per episode.

## Mute the noise

You can mute a show, a topic, a domain or a user. A muted show stops appearing in your lists. If a host who used to be good has drifted, mute the feed and move on. Nothing is deleted from your account.

## Find shows through people

Search by what you want to know about, then look at who recommends it.

- Join a [community](https://voucha.ai/communities) in a subject you care about. Owners and moderators keep [community-curated lists](https://voucha.ai/communities?has_list_items=true) of sources, and podcasts can be on them.
- Follow people whose taste fits yours. Their posts and links show up in your feed.
- Browse [news](https://voucha.ai/news) sources and feeds, and follow the shows that look right.

A recommendation on Voucha is a post with a vote behind it. A vote from a long-standing, verified account counts for more than one from a day-old account. That keeps a popular show with a loud fan club from burying a smaller one. See [Mentioned Is Not Recommended](./mentioned-vs-recommended.md).

## Where to start

1. Add three shows you already listen to.
2. Join one community about something you want to learn.
3. Follow two people from it.
4. Look at what they added to the community's lists.
