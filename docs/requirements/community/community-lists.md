# Community Lists

See also: [community_list actions](../reference-communitylist.md) · [Community Lists lifecycle](../reference-community-lists.md)

Communities can maintain curated lists of entities—topics, RSS feeds, posts, domains, and URLs. Community leaders use lists to direct members toward important content. Members can "proxy follow" or "proxy mute" an entire list, applying one action to all items at once.
