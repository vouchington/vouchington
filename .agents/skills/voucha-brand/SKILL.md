# Voucha Brand Voice

## Voice rules

- No em-dashes (—) anywhere in user-facing copy. Use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses instead.
- Short, plain, concrete sentences. Aim for one idea per sentence.
- Cut filler words: "actually", "truly", "seamlessly", "unlock", "empower", "discover".
- Avoid the "X — not Y" construction. Prefer two separate sentences.
- Avoid rule-of-three clichés ("fast, simple, and powerful").
- Name concrete content types: news, podcasts, videos, reviews, referral links. Don't say "content".
- Every piece of marketing copy should link to the relevant intent or page (see Linking map below).
- Keep headings noun-first, not verb-first ("The Platform" not "Get The Platform").

## Positioning

- Trust is vouched and people-first, not algorithmic.
- We connect you straight to the source. No middlemen, no summaries.
- AI connects people to content — it does not summarize for them.
- "Your circle of trust" is a key metaphor. Use it.

## Key phrases (use verbatim when relevant)

- "The Social Trust Network"
- "people and sources you trust"
- "your circle of trust"
- "vouched community"
- "straight to the source"
- "AI to connect us"
- "we prefer connecting you to your friends and trusted sources over summarizing for you"
- "referral links from your inner circle"
- "topics as votable tags"
- "community-driven labeling"
- "community-curated lists"

## Linking map

## Examples

**Instead of:**

> Follow the people and sources you actually trust. Voucha brings you their news, reviews, and recommendations — and keeps out the fake reviews, paid placements, and AI slop.

**Write:**

> News, podcasts, videos, reviews, and referral links from people and sources you trust.

---

**Instead of:**

> An AI that only trusts your sources.

**Write:**

> AI to connect us. We prefer connecting you to your friends and trusted sources over summarizing for you.

---

**Instead of:**

> Build your own vouched community — with trust and moderation built in.

**Write:**

> Build your own vouched community. Trust and moderation built in from day one.

## Checklist before publishing copy

1. No em-dashes?
2. No filler words ("actually", "truly", "seamless")?
3. Concrete content types named (not "content")?
4. Relevant links included?
5. Short sentences (aim for under 15 words each)?
6. Key phrases used where appropriate?
